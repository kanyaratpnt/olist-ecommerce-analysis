# olist-ecommerce-analysis

## Key Insights & Business Recommendations

### 1. Sales Declined Significantly in the Second Half of the Year

Sales declined noticeably after the mid-year period, with September and October recording the lowest sales levels of the year—more than 50% below the peak period. Although sales recovered slightly in November, they declined again in December, indicating considerable sales volatility toward the end of the year.

**Business Recommendation**

Plan promotional campaigns in advance for September–October and December to stimulate demand and mitigate the impact of low-sales periods.

---

### 2. Revenue Is Highly Concentrated in São Paulo (SP)

São Paulo (SP) accounted for 38% of total revenue across 27 states and generated nearly three times the revenue of the second-ranked state, Rio de Janeiro (RJ). This indicates a high level of dependence on a single core market, which could significantly impact overall revenue if demand in São Paulo declines.

**Business Recommendation**

Expand marketing activities and promotional campaigns in secondary markets such as RJ, MG, and RS to reduce reliance on a single market and build a more diversified customer base.

---

### 3. Delivery Times Increased from Late Year to Early Year

The average delivery time across the year was approximately 12 days and increased to around 14–16 days between November and March. In addition, 7.91% of successfully delivered orders were classified as late. While the available data does not establish a direct causal relationship between longer delivery times and declining sales, the trend highlights an area of delivery performance that requires further investigation.

**Business Recommendation**

Review logistics operations and delivery partners during the November–March period to improve delivery consistency. Further analysis should also examine the relationship between delivery delays and customer repeat purchase behavior.
